# Workflow 04: Data Principal rights requests

Last checked: 28 October 2026. Educational, not legal advice. Fictional company: BasketBay.

> **STATUS: DPDP preparation, not a current DPDP duty.** The DPDP obligations this workflow supports are notified but **not yet in force** (main duties computed 13 May 2027; Consent Manager provisions computed 13 November 2026). No DPDP step here is mandatory today. Duties under other laws already in force - for example CERT-In's 6-hour cyber-incident reporting for covered entities (directions of 28 April 2022) - are unaffected by this status note. The CSV/register templates in this folder are **illustrative internal tools - not statutory or prescribed forms**; the DPDP Rules prescribe the content and timing of breach intimations (Rule 7), not a fixed form.


**Legal hook:** DPDP Act 2023, ss.11-14 (rights), s.13 (grievance redressal), s.15 (duties of Data Principals). Not yet in force; computed commencement 13 May 2027. The Act requires a readily available grievance mechanism; response timelines are set by the Rules - check the current Rule text before quoting a number.

## The four rights
1. **Access** (s.11): summary of personal data, processing activities, identities of others it was shared with.
2. **Correction, completion, updating, erasure** (s.12).
3. **Grievance redressal** (s.13): the Data Fiduciary must respond within the published period.
4. **Nomination** (s.14): nominate someone to exercise rights on death or incapacity.

## Steps
1. Publish one visible request channel (in-app + email) and name the officer who answers.
2. Log every request on receipt: type, identity-verification step, due date.
3. Verify identity proportionately - enough to stop impostors, not so much it becomes a barrier.
4. Fulfil from the inventory (workflow 01): the register tells you where the data lives, including with processors.
5. Record the response and any refusal with reasons; refusals need a lawful basis, not preference.
6. Track the grievance clock; unanswered grievances are what reach the Board.

## Fictional example: BasketBay
A customer asks for "everything you have on me". The DSR log opens, identity is verified against the account phone, the inventory register points to 3 systems, and the answer goes out as one readable summary listing data held, purposes and the two processors, inside the published response window.

## Template
dsr-log.csv - one row per request.
