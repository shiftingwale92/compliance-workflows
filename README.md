# compliance-workflows

Step-by-step compliance workflows, checklists and register templates for Indian law, built as a **learning portfolio**. Every example uses the fictional company **BasketBay** (an imaginary Bengaluru D2C grocery startup). No real client, employer or customer data appears anywhere.

**Educational material, not legal advice.** Rules cited here are checked against official sources on the "last checked" date in each workflow, and Indian laws commence in stages: each workflow says whether the obligation it describes is in force, notified-but-not-yet-operative, or in draft. Verify the current text before relying on anything.

## What the templates are - and are not
The CSV registers, logs and checklists here are **illustrative internal templates for learning and preparation**. They are **not statutory or prescribed forms**, and no Indian regulator currently requires these exact documents. Where a regulator prescribes a reporting format (for example, CERT-In's incident reporting format for covered entities), that format controls. The DPDP Rules prescribe the content and timing of breach intimations (Rule 7), not a fixed form. The DPDP compliance steps described are **voluntary preparation for obligations that are notified but not yet in force** - they are not current DPDP duties. Duties under other laws already in force - for example CERT-In's 6-hour cyber-incident reporting for covered entities - are unaffected by that status.
